# Incident Response Plan

## 1. Purpose
An Incident Response Plan defines the actions an organization should take when a cybersecurity incident is suspected or confirmed.

## 2. Objectives
- Detect incidents early.
- Reduce impact and prevent further compromise.
- Define roles and escalation.
- Preserve useful evidence.
- Contain, eradicate, and recover safely.
- Document lessons learned and improvements.

## 3. Scope
Covers endpoints, servers, networks, cloud services, applications, identities, business data, and users.

## 4. Response Team
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Incident Response Lead | Coordinates incident response |
| SOC/Security Analyst | Triage, monitoring, analysis |
| IT Administrator | Isolation and recovery |
| Network Administrator | Network investigation and blocking |
| Forensics | Evidence collection |
| Management | Business decisions and escalation |
| Legal/Privacy | Compliance and notification |
| Communications/HR | Approved communications |

## 5. Severity
- **Critical:** major business impact or widespread compromise.
- **High:** significant risk or multiple systems/users affected.
- **Medium:** limited but confirmed security incident.
- **Low:** minor or unconfirmed event.

## 6. Lifecycle
1. Preparation
2. Detection and Analysis
3. Response / Containment
4. Eradication
5. Recovery
6. Lessons Learned and Continuous Improvement

## 7. Procedure
1. Report suspected event.
2. Triage and validate.
3. Declare and classify incident.
4. Preserve evidence.
5. Contain affected systems/accounts.
6. Investigate scope and root cause.
7. Eradicate malicious activity.
8. Recover from trusted systems/backups.
9. Communicate with authorized stakeholders.
10. Close incident and track improvements.

## 8. Evidence
Record incident ID, timestamps, affected assets, users, alerts, logs, IPs/domains, hashes, screenshots, actions, and chain-of-custody information when required.

## 9. Recovery
Validate that malicious activity is removed, credentials are secured, systems are patched, backups are trusted, monitoring is active, and business functions work normally.

## 10. Continuous Improvement
Perform a post-incident review, assign corrective actions, update procedures, and test the plan periodically.

# Week 4 – Security Awareness Campaign Planning

## Internship Task
**Security Awareness Campaign Planning**

This repository contains my Week 4 cybersecurity internship submission. The project develops a practical security awareness campaign for a hypothetical organization.

## Objective
The objective is to educate employees about cybersecurity best practices, reduce human-related security risk, improve reporting behavior, and measure awareness improvement.

## Organization
**ApexNova Technologies** – a hypothetical organization with approximately 150 employees across IT, Finance, HR, Sales, Operations, and Management.

## Campaign Theme
**STOP. CHECK. REPORT.**

- **STOP** – Do not immediately click, reply, pay, download, or share.
- **CHECK** – Verify the sender, request, link, attachment, identity, and context.
- **REPORT** – Use the approved security-reporting channel for suspicious activity.

## Key Awareness Topics
1. Phishing and social engineering
2. Password security and MFA
3. Email and web safety
4. Data protection
5. Device security
6. Remote-work security
7. Incident reporting
8. Physical security

## Four-Week Plan

| Week | Focus | Main Activities |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phishing & Social Engineering | Kick-off, micro-learning, examples, quiz |
| 2 | Passwords, MFA & Accounts | Training, MFA tips, password guidance, quiz |
| 3 | Data, Devices & Remote Work | Training, secure-work checklist, examples |
| 4 | Incident Reporting & Reinforcement | Reporting exercise, approved phishing simulation, final quiz |

## Communication Methods
- Kick-off email
- Weekly micro-learning
- Posters/digital signage
- Security tips
- Quizzes
- Approved simulated phishing exercise
- Manager briefings
- Final assessment and feedback survey

## Success Metrics
- Training completion ≥ 95%
- Average quiz score ≥ 80%
- Reduced phishing click rate from baseline
- Increased phishing reporting rate
- ≥ 90% know how to report incidents
- ≥ 80% positive usefulness feedback

## Estimated Project Effort
**31 hours**, within the requested 30–35 hour range.

The campaign combines awareness education, practical communication, behavioral reinforcement, incident reporting, controlled simulation, measurable objectives, and continuous improvement. It is designed to be realistic, clear, and implementable for a hypothetical organization.

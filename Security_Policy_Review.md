# Security Policy Review

## Objective
Evaluate a hypothetical organization's cybersecurity policy, identify gaps, assess risk, and recommend practical improvements.

## Key Findings
- Authentication: require MFA for privileged, remote-access, and sensitive accounts.
- Access Control: apply least privilege, role-based access, and periodic access reviews.
- Offboarding: promptly disable accounts and remove permissions.
- Data Protection: classify sensitive data and apply appropriate encryption/access controls.
- Endpoint Security: centrally manage endpoint protection and patching.
- Network Security: maintain firewalls, segmentation, secure remote access, and monitoring.
- Email Security: strengthen phishing protection and reporting.
- Awareness: conduct recurring employee security training.
- Incident Response: define reporting, escalation, evidence, and recovery procedures.
- Backup: maintain protected backups and regularly test restoration.
- Third Parties: assess vendors and restrict external access.
- Logging: centralize important security logs.
- Governance: assign an owner, review regularly, and manage exceptions.

## Improvement Plan
### 0–30 days
MFA, offboarding, endpoint protection, incident reporting, backup validation.

### 31–90 days
Access reviews, data classification, logging, awareness training, network improvements.

### 3–6 months
Vendor security, policy governance, exercises, recovery testing.

### Ongoing
Annual policy review, metrics, tabletop exercises, control testing, and continuous improvement.
